Framework Engineering

Kevin's work started with a deceptively difficult question: if AI can generate software faster than ever, what engineering system has to exist around it for the result to deserve trust? That question has grown into EDF and Clarity for diagnosis and reasoning; Ordo and Praxis for state-directed engineering and governed execution; Limen for browser and WebAssembly boundaries; Aegis and Tutela for fault and security reasoning; Forma and Folio for presentation; Percepta and Dokimos for experience and quality verification; and Conditor for repeatable installation and lifecycle management.

The systems are exercised through real application work including HelixNote, Signal, Chrona, Summa, and Strata. Nothing gets a free pass because it looks elegant on paper. The methods have to survive requirements, code, verification, accessibility, security, operational faults, evidence, and change over time.
